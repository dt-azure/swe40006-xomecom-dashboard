# Xóm E-Com
## Multi-region B2C e-commerce

Xóm E-Com - Global E-Commerce Platform
Xóm E-Com is an e-commerce marketplace operating in more than 140 countries across 4 main markets: US, EMEA, APAC, LATAM. Unlike Xóm Retail (physical stores), Xóm E-Com has no brick-and-mortar locations - every transaction happens via web + app, shipped directly to the customer's address through logistics partners.

Business model
Revenue is ~$2.3M/year, gross margin ~20% (lower than retail due to logistics fees + aggressive discounts). The catalog spans 3 major categories: Office Supplies, Furniture, Technology with ~1,900 SKUs. Customers are split into 3 segments: Consumer (individuals, 60% of volume), Corporate (B2B, 25%), Home Office (SMB, 15%). Each segment has different buying behavior.

Pain points
Discount cannibalization - marketing runs heavy discounts (20-80%) to drive volume but no one measures whether discounts eat into profit. There are orders discounted 80% with negative profit.
Segment profitability - the C-suite suspects Corporate is the cash cow, but the CFO has never built a P&L by segment.
Geographic expansion - Xóm E-Com wants to expand into more markets and needs to know which current markets are growing and which are saturating.
Customer LTV gap - most customers buy once then disappear.
